Try it yourself · 3 minutes · works on your phone

1. Open the supplier portal, change any value, click Issue & Sign — your browser signs the record with a demo key (a real mill's key never leaves its own vault).

2. Scan the QR with your phone. The QR carries the signed record itself; the verifier checks the signature against the public issuer registry — no account, no app, no server.

3. On the verifier, click Tamper to watch a forged edit get caught by the math, Compare to paper to catch an altered printout, then go back and issue a correction to see how an honest typo is fixed without hiding it.
